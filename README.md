# The Hollow Descent

> A dark fantasy roguelike dungeon crawler.
> Descend ten floors. Prove your worthiness.
> Find your father.

---

## What Is This

The Hollow Descent is a Pygame-based roguelike dungeon crawler built in Python.
You play as Vincent, a young man who discovers a door beneath his family farm
leading into an ancient structure called the Underspire. Each of the ten floors
is a network of self-contained rooms explored one at a time. Every floor is
sealed until you find and satisfy its unique requirement — forcing genuine
exploration before descent. Combat is turn-based, death is permanent, and every
run is procedurally generated.
